# Human-Heart-Cell-Atlas

The Human Heart Cell Atlas (HHCA) is a community-driven, integrated reference of cellular diversity in the healthy human heart. It combines single-nucleus RNA sequencing data from 15 studies, covering eight anatomical regions and approximately 1.5 million nuclei from 477 samples across 280 individuals.

All datasets were reprocessed using standardized analytical pipelines, with harmonized technical and donor metadata. Through iterative, consensus-based annotation, the HHCA defines 100 cardiac cell states across four levels of resolution, including rare cell populations.

The atlas enables population-scale analysis of cardiac cellular phenotypes and reveals age- and sex-associated molecular signatures. Cell identities, molecular programs, and tissue localization were validated using matched spatial transcriptomics together with spatially resolved and single-cell proteomics data.

By providing a unified, harmonized, and open-access reference of human cardiac cell types across diverse populations, the HHCA establishes a shared framework for studying heart biology and disease. The spatially extended atlas also supports translational applications, including drug discovery, analysis of neurovascular organization, characterization of immune and cytokine responses, and rapid clinical contextualization in acute heart transplant rejection.
